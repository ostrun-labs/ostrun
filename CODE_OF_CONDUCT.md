# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be respectful, stay on topic. This project is open to external contributions; it should stay a place where anyone can propose a service, report a bug, or ask a question without fear of being put down.

## Expected behavior

- Stay factual and constructive in issues, PRs, and discussions, even when disagreeing.
- Accept technical criticism of your code without taking it personally, and phrase your own the same way.
- Respect the maintainer's and other contributors' time: give clear context in issues/PRs (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`), no aggressive follow-ups.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, discriminatory or harassing remarks, regardless of motive.
- Deliberate trolling, provocation, or derailing an issue/PR from its topic.
- Sharing someone else's private information without their consent (doxing).
- Spam or off-topic promotion in issues/PRs/discussions.

## Enforcement

To report a conduct problem, open an issue if the topic can be discussed in public. If it can't, open an issue that only asks the maintainer for a private channel, without the details. The maintainer may, at their discretion:

- edit or remove a problematic comment/issue/PR,
- warn the person involved,
- temporarily or permanently ban someone from the repo for repeat or serious violations.

There's no dedicated moderation team for now. The project is maintained by a single person on their own time. This will be revisited if the project grows.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official project space (the `ostrun`, `ostrun-auth-service`, `ostrun-messaging-service` repos, and any future officially catalogued repo).
